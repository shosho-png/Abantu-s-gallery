Two ways to work with us.

For a specific research project, we build public‑facing work together with you and the people your research affects.

  • PhotoVoice and visual storytelling
  • Sonic ethnography and audio essays
  • Video and design for public audiences

Training and advice

For humanities and social science researchers who want to run participatory, arts‑based dissemination themselves.

  • Workshops for honours, master’s, PhD and postdoctoral researchers
  • Advice on building dissemination into funding proposals

Why this exists.

Socially relevant research is often held inside institutions most people cannot enter. Existing science communication in South Africa leans toward STEM. Humanities and social science researchers have little support to share their work in ways the public can use.

We work from three commitments: decolonising knowledge systems, quality education and reduced inequality (SDG 4 and 10), and two‑way conversation between experts and the public.

Informed consent covers creative outputs, platforms and revenue. Ownership, credit and community benefit are agreed before production begins.

About Us

Abantu's Gallery is a science communication consultancy founded by Melusi Gumede and Shongile Myeza. We specialise in participatory visual, sonic and arts-based methods for social science and humanities research in South Africa.

Our Approach

We bridge the gap between experts and the public through innovative, accessible communication approaches. Our work is grounded in decolonising knowledge systems and maintaining participatory two-way communication.

Our Team

Melusi Gumede and Shongile Myeza bring together expertise in research methods, visual storytelling, audio production, and creative design to make research accessible and engaging.
